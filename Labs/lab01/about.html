About Satoru Iwata

Short Biography

Satoru Iwata was born in Sapporo, Japan. Since high school, he loved programming and saved money from his part-time job washing dishes to buy a programmable calculator. Later, he studied Computer Science at the Tokyo Institute of Technology and joined a small game studio called HAL Laboratory while still in college.

As a person, Iwata was famous for being humble, kind, and deeply empathetic. He represented the heart of the gaming industry: when the Wii U console had low sales, he refused to fire his employees because he believed workers could not create fun games if they were afraid of losing their jobs. Instead, he cut his own salary by 50%.

Important Facts

  • Born: December 6, 1959 (Sapporo, Japan)
  • Died: July 11, 2015 (Kyoto, Japan)
  • Education: Tokyo Institute of Technology
  • Companies: HAL Laboratory and Nintendo
  • Famous Quote: "On my business card, I am a corporate president. In my mind, I am a game developer. But in my heart, I am a gamer."

Career Highlights

  1. 1980: Started working at HAL Laboratory as a part-time programmer.
  2. 1993: Promoted to President of HAL Laboratory and saved the company from heavy debt.
  3. 2000: Joined Nintendo as the head of its corporate planning division.
  4. 2002: Became the 4th President of Nintendo, leading the Nintendo DS and Wii eras.
  5. 2011: Created the Nintendo Direct format to communicate directly with fans.
