Important Works

Here are 4 of Satoru Iwata's most important works and contributions to the gaming world:

1. Pokémon Gold and Silver (1999)

When Game Freak was developing Pokémon Gold and Silver for the Game Boy Color, the game data was too big for the cartridge. Iwata programmed a brilliant graphic compression tool that saved so much space that the developers were able to include the entire Kanto region from the first game.


2. Nintendo Direct Broadcasts (2011 - 2015)

Iwata created and hosted Nintendo Direct, a series of online presentations where he spoke directly to players around the world with his iconic "Direct to you!" hand gesture, changing how video game companies announce their games forever.


3. Development of the Nintendo Switch

Before his passing in 2015, Iwata led the planning and concept design of Nintendo's next console, codenamed "NX". His vision of a hybrid console that could be played both on the TV and as a portable system became the Nintendo Switch, one of the best-selling consoles in history.


4. Ask Iwata: Words of Wisdom (Book)

Based on his famous interview series "Iwata Asks", this book compiles his thoughts, philosophy on game design, and lessons on leadership and kindness.
