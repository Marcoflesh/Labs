My 3 Favorite Works by Satoru Iwata

Out of everything Satoru Iwata programmed, created, and led during his life, these are my 3 personal favorites and why I chose them:

1. Pokémon Gold

Why I chose it: I chose Pokémon Gold because it shows Iwata's genius as a programmer. Even when he was already an important executive, he sat down to read the code and created a compression algorithm so players could travel back to Kanto after beating Johto. Discovering that second region is one of the best moments in gaming history.

2. The Nintendo Switch Concept

Why I chose it: I chose the development of the Nintendo Switch because it was his final gift to gamers. He understood that people wanted to play great console games anywhere, and his work on the "NX" project saved Nintendo and gave us an amazing generation of games.

3. His GDC 2005 Speech & "Ask Iwata" Philosophy

Why I chose it: More than a single game, my favorite thing is his philosophy and what he represented in the gaming industry. When he said his mythical quote: "On my business card, I am a corporate president. In my mind, I am a game developer. But in my heart, I am a gamer," he proved that you can be the head of a giant company and still be a kind, passionate person who truly loves video games and respects players.
