Satoru Iwata

Introduction

Welcome to my tribute page for Satoru Iwata (1959 - 2015). He was a legendary Japanese video game programmer, developer, and the 4th President of Nintendo.

Unlike traditional business executives, Iwata started his career writing code and making games with his own hands. His visual style and philosophy were all about simplicity, innovation, and making video games fun for everyone in the family.
